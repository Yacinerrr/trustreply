# Access Control Policy

## Authentication

All employees must use multi-factor authentication (MFA) for email, source code hosting
and the production cloud console. Passwords must be at least 12 characters long.

## Access reviews

Managers review user access to production systems every quarter. Accounts belonging to
people who leave the company are disabled within 24 hours.

## Privileged access

Administrator rights are granted only to named engineers and are logged.
